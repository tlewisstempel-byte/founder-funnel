# Wednesday founder content chat (one meeting)

**Purpose:** one 25-30 minute session each Wednesday with Floris that does two jobs: **pick next week's posts** from the ideas board, and **capture raw material** (his views, stories, reactions) that becomes future posts. Floris talks; the system writes.

**When and format:** Wednesdays, Google Meet, recorded with transcript on. Save the transcript as `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (or paste it into the bot later), then run `prompts/meeting-to-ideas.md`; the ideas it finds go onto the board.

## Before the meeting (B2B lead, 10 min, Wednesday morning)
- The bot's **ideas board digest** arrives in Slack (top 10 from every source, scored). Pre-tick your suggested 4-5.
- Check `/buffer`: how many approved drafts are banked? Aim for 8-10.
- Note the 1-2 live themes the board flags (news scan).
- Bring 1 data result if one exists (anonymised and approved).
- Log any numbers still due (`/metrics`): the digest opens with **Last week's results**, best first.

## Agenda (30 min)
| Min | Section | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | **Last week:** walk the digest's results (best first): what landed, what didn't, anything he'd change | Notes for the learning loop |
| 3-10 | **Pick next week:** walk the board's top ideas; Floris confirms or swaps your 4-5; agree the angle in one line each | **Confirm picks** in Slack; bot drafts them |
| 10-17 | **The week in the sector:** his take on the live themes | Raw material for policy takes |
| 17-24 | **Inside Stairpay:** one story, one lesson, one thing customers said; react to one data point | Story and data posts |
| 24-30 | **Quick fire:** 3-5 one-line opinions (below) | Hooks and comment material |

**After (B2B lead, 10 min):** confirm picks in Slack (if not done live), run the transcript prompt, drop the top new ideas into the board with `/idea`, and anything timely into `/draft`.

## Question bank (pick, don't do them all)

**Policy and market**
- What's the sector getting wrong about [theme] this week?
- If you were housing minister for a day, what one thing would you change in shared ownership?
- What will providers regret not doing in the next 12 months?
- Who's doing shared ownership well right now, and why? (no names needed on the record)

**Building Stairpay**
- What decision did you make this month that you'd have got wrong two years ago?
- What's a customer conversation from this week that stuck with you?
- What's harder than people think about selling software to housing associations?
- What did the team do this week that you're proud of?
- What's something you've changed your mind about?

**Data**
- Does this number surprise you? What did you expect?
- What would a head of home ownership do differently if they knew this?
- What's the question this data can't answer yet?

**Quick fire (one line each)**
- Staircasing in one sentence, for a housing association CEO.
- The most overrated idea in shared ownership.
- One thing you'd tell a provider launching their first resales process.
- Finish this: "Shared ownership works when…"

## Rules
- Picking and raw material are one meeting; don't split them unless the session overruns two weeks running.
- Record only with consent; nothing customer-identifying goes into a post without written OK.
- His exact phrases matter more than his summaries: the transcript is the voice data.
- Anything timely goes to the fast lane the same day (`/draft`).
