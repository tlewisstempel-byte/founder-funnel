# Weekly Floris meeting kit

**Purpose:** 15-30 minutes a week that become 3-5 posts. Floris talks; the system writes. This is the single biggest input for sounding like him.

**When:** Wednesdays, alongside the founder content review (to confirm whether they are the same session).

**Format:** Google Meet, recorded, transcript on. Save the transcript as `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (or paste it into the bot later), then run `prompts/meeting-to-ideas.md`.

## Before the meeting (B2B lead, 10 min)
- Check `/buffer`: how many approved drafts are banked? Aim for 8-10.
- Pick 2 "live themes" from `trends/watchlist.md` and any news this week.
- Bring 1 data result if one exists (anonymised and approved).
- Bring last week's best and worst post, with numbers.

## Agenda (25 min)
| Min | Section | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | **Last week:** what landed, what didn't, anything he'd change | Feeds the learning loop |
| 3-10 | **The week in the sector:** his take on the 2 live themes | Policy takes need his view, not ours |
| 10-17 | **Inside Stairpay:** one story, one lesson, one thing customers said | Story and proof posts |
| 17-22 | **Data:** react to one number: surprised? why? so what for providers? | Data posts need his "so what" |
| 22-25 | **Quick fire:** 3-5 one-line opinions (below) | Hooks and comments material |

## Question bank (pick, don't do them all)

**Policy and market**
- What's the sector getting wrong about [theme] this week?
- If you were housing minister for a day, what one thing would you change in shared ownership?
- What will providers regret not doing in the next 12 months?
- Who's doing shared ownership well right now, and why? (no names needed on the record)

**Building Stairpay**
- What decision did you make this month that you'd have got wrong two years ago?
- What's a customer conversation from this week that stuck with you?
- What's harder than people think about selling software to housing associations?
- What did the team do this week that you're proud of?
- What's something you've changed your mind about?

**Data**
- Does this number surprise you? What did you expect?
- What would a head of home ownership do differently if they knew this?
- What's the question this data can't answer yet?

**Quick fire (one line each)**
- Staircasing in one sentence, for a housing association CEO.
- The most overrated idea in shared ownership.
- One thing you'd tell a provider launching their first resales process.
- Finish this: "Shared ownership works when…"

## Rules
- Record only with consent; nothing customer-identifying goes into a post without written OK.
- His exact phrases matter more than his summaries: the transcript is the voice data.
- Anything timely goes to the fast lane the same day (`/draft`).
