# Owner's edits: October 2026

The B2B lead's edits are a training signal too (Floris's edits go in the monthly file). Source: income-caps draft, 29 Oct 2026, Claude version A vs owner rewrite.

## Patterns
- **Articles:** avoid repeating "the" where a possessive or nothing reads more naturally ("once they have *the* keys" → "once they have *their* keys"). Repeated definite articles read as machine-written.
- **Cut the extra beat:** dropped "Getting people in has never been the difficult part. It's the years afterwards." Once the proof point lands ("1 in 3 staircasing transactions…"), stop; don't add a second rhetorical turn.
- **No callbacks for the sake of it:** dropped the "A month ago I wrote about Help to Buy…" paragraph. Keep one argument per post.
- **No crafted parallel closer:** replaced "The caps decide how many people get in. What happens next decides how many of them ever own their home outright." with a plain mission statement: "…it's our mission at Stairpay to help as many people as possible through that entire journey."
- **Break dense paragraphs:** the 250,000 fact and the owners' question became separate paragraphs.
- **Shorter overall:** about 30% fewer words than the draft.

## Proposed changes to the drafting rules
- Prefer possessives or no article over repeated "the".
- One rhetorical turn per post; end on the mission or a plain statement, not a constructed aphorism.
- Fact and implication in separate short paragraphs.
