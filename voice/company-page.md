# Voice: Stairpay company page

Derived from the Stairpay design kit's voice rules (https://claude.ai/artifact/CF5UhbgmPgd5BbA8Jha4cA). **Caveat:** the kit describes the consumer-facing brand (a free service for shared owners). The B2B voice for the company page is still to be confirmed; until then, use these rules as the baseline.

## Rules
- Plain English, short sentences; explain a term the first time ("staircasing = buying a bigger share of your home").
- Sentence-case headlines; a full stop is fine on a statement.
- British English and pounds: £129,850, 45%, "per month".
- Reassure, never hype: no exclamation marks, no emoji, no urgency, no superlatives.
- Stay factual. Stairpay provides information, not financial or legal advice; never promise outcomes.

## When the company page posts
Key posts only (product launches, flagship data, partner announcements). Floris's post leads; the company page version follows the same day in this voice and links back to his post where appropriate.

## Open question
Does the company page need a more provider-facing register than the kit's resident-facing one? Decide with Floris and log it in `decisions/DECISIONS.md`.
