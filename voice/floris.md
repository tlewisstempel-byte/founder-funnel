## Observed so far (small sample)

- **Hooks:** a direct question to the audience ("Any hockey fans out here?"), a counter-intuitive statement, or a short line announcing a topic.
- **Structure:** short paragraphs; short bullet lists for arguments; a chart or photo carries many posts.
- **Emoji:** used as inline markers, including at the end of bullet lines (chart, wrestlers) and in a post opener. Frequent.
- **Exclamation marks:** used for enthusiasm ("Wow! What a proud moment...").
- **Hashtags:** brand and campaign tags (#SharedOwnership, #TogetherForHomes), used sparingly.
- **Person and tone:** first-person plural for company ("we", "our"), plain and warm, willing to take a position on policy.
- **Topics seen:** growth data, policy (equity loan scheme vs shared ownership), product launch (end-to-end shared ownership CRM), team life, partners and podcasts, causes and events.
