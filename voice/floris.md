- **Hooks:** a counter-intuitive one-liner ("The least obvious thing to do when things are going well is to stop.") or a playful question with emoji ("Did you say double or nothing?? 2️⃣❎").
- **Numbers up front and concrete:** 75,000 homes, tripled ARR, £50,000 raised, 1,000 runners, £100,000 goal.
- **Honest, self-aware turn:** "On paper, that's amazing, but…"; "we're still far from making a dent in the housing crisis". He undercuts his own wins rather than hyping them.
- **Mission language:** "increase the supply of affordable homes… by increasing transparency into affordable housing and improving operational efficiency".
- **Sentences:** conversational and mid-length, joined with commas and "but"; not clipped fragments. Plain words.
- **Structure:** short paragraphs, then a numbered or dashed list when there are several points.
- **Tags inline:** the company (Stairpay), partners (Crisis) and colleagues (Daniel Larbi) are tagged mid-sentence; light humour in brackets ("ask Daniel if you've not heard that word before 😬").
- **Emoji:** one or two per post, at the end of a line as punctuation (💜 🤖 😬 🍕 🍻), never mid-sentence.
- **Capitalisation:** writes "Shared Ownership" and "Shared Owners" with capitals; "SO" as shorthand once the term is established.
- **Hashtags:** one inline where it reads naturally (#SharedOwnership), or two to three lower-case tags at the end (#fundraising #charity #housing).
- **Endings:** an invitation ("Want to be involved? Let me know!") or a collective line ("Together, the housing sector can…").
- **Exclamation marks:** used, but in campaign/celebration posts more than in business posts.
