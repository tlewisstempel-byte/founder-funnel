?? How much longer can your organisation live with messy siloed data? 

My entire LinkedIn feed is peppered with posts on AI, so here's my take. 

? AI can't help you if it can't fetch the right information. Start with cleaning your data or you're wasting your money.

Here's what we hear a lot:

"I've submitted a ticket to get rental information from IT"

"We've been through a few mergers so we can't access that information"

"I need to check Dynamics and the sales CRM as they don't match"

"Can you integrate with (_insert software name built in the 1980s_)"

? Based on what I've seen from the organisations I've spoken to in the sector there is very little point in investing heavily in AI that touches operational staff until you sort out the data first.
