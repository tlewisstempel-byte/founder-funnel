# Playbook: story / people / behind the scenes

**Why:** this is where Floris sounds most human and where trust is built. Team moments and founder lessons keep him top of mind between data and policy posts.

## When to use
A team moment, a lesson, a hiring or milestone note, an event, a counter-intuitive decision ("the least obvious thing to do when things are going well is to stop" is the kind of idea that works here).

## Inputs
- Real photo or video from Floris or the team (via Slack)
- A few lines from Floris, ideally from the weekly meeting

## Draft structure
1. **Hook:** a short, human line
2. **The moment:** what happened, in plain terms
3. **The lesson or why it matters**, in one or two lines
4. **Close:** invite a reply, or end cleanly

## Visual
Real photos only. Consent from anyone who is identifiable. No stock images.

## Team funnel
| Who | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| People in the post | Comment, only if natural | within 60 min |
| Reposts | None (they make personal posts feel staged) | |
| Company page | No | |

## Don'ts
- No humble-bragging templates
- No engagement bait
- Nothing about customers or colleagues without their OK
