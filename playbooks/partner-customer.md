# Playbook: partner / customer proof

**Why:** third-party credibility. Floris's 4 Aug podcast post with Southern Housing is an example of the format.

## When to use
A podcast, joint event, case study, quote or win involving a partner or customer.

## Hard requirement
**Written OK from the named organisation before drafting.** If there is no OK, write it anonymised ("a large housing provider") or don't write it.

## Inputs
- What happened and why it matters to the sector
- Written approval and the preferred way to tag them
- A real photo or clip

## Draft structure
1. **Hook:** the insight or result, not the logo
2. **The story:** what we did together, in plain terms
3. **What others can learn**
4. **Credit:** tag the partner and the individuals involved (with OK)

## Team funnel
| Who | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Partner | Heads-up before posting; invite to comment or share | before T-0 |
| B2B lead | Comment | within 60 min |
| Tim | Comment if product-relevant | |
| Company page | Yes if it is a key post | same day |
| Reposts | Optional, with a comment | day 1 |

## Don'ts
- No naming without written OK
- No overclaiming results
