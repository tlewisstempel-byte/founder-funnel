# Playbook: product / launch

**Why:** product moments need the whole team behind them. Floris's 7 Oct CRM video post (41 reactions, 4 comments, 2 reposts) is the baseline to beat.

## When to use
A feature launch, a new capability, a customer-visible milestone. Flag as **key post**: company page version included.

## Inputs
- What changed, who it's for, one concrete before/after
- Demo video or screenshots
- Quote or proof point (anonymised data or a named customer with written OK)

## Draft structure
1. **Hook:** the problem it removes, not the feature name
2. **What's new:** one or two lines
3. **Proof:** a data point, outcome or quote
4. **Who it helps** and how to find out more (link in first comment)

## Visual
Short demo clip or on-brand screenshot card. Brand kit applies fully.

## Team funnel (full)
| Who | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Tim | Repost with own comment (system drafts 2-3 lines in his voice) | within 60 min |
| B2B lead | Repost with own comment | within 60 min |
| Sinead | Repost with own comment from the shared-owner side | within 60 min |
| Wider team | Optional repost with a comment (plain reposts get little reach) | day 1 |
| Company page | Own post in company-page voice; Floris's post is the lead | same day |

## Don'ts
- No superlatives or hype
- No claims about outcomes we can't evidence
- No financial or legal advice
