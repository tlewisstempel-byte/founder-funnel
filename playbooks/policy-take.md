# Playbook: policy / market take

**Why:** timely, opinionated posts build authority. Floris's 28 Sep equity loan scheme post (37 reactions) is the model: list the live arguments, then say what's missing.

## When to use
A government announcement, consultation, sector report or market move that affects shared ownership or housing providers. Uses the **fast lane**: draft within hours, same-day approval requested.

## Inputs
- Source link(s) (official first)
- A Stairpay data point where one exists
- Floris's view (from meeting, voice note or Slack reply)

## Draft structure
1. **Hook:** what happened, in one line
2. **The arguments:** the two or three live debates (short list)
3. **What's missing:** the point few are making, ideally backed by Stairpay data
4. **Floris's view:** one clear sentence
5. **Close:** a question to the sector

## Rules
- Comment on **issues and schemes**, not named politicians (default; open for discussion)
- Facts checked against the primary source
- Stay out of party politics; criticise policy design, not people

## Visual
Usually none, or a single clean stat card if one number carries the argument.

## Don'ts
- No speculation presented as fact
- No posting without Floris's approval, even when timely
