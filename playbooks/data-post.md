# Playbook: data post

**Why:** our biggest unfair advantage. Few in the sector can publish real, anonymised platform data. Floris's August growth chart (37 reactions, 4 comments) is the format to scale.

## When to use
A figure, trend or comparison from the anonymised feed that a housing provider would want to know or argue about.

## Inputs
- Approved anonymised query result, with period and sample size
- One-line "so what" from Floris or the owner
- Optional: a related policy moment or Floris opinion

## Draft structure
1. **Hook:** the number or the surprise, in the first two lines
2. **Context:** one or two lines on what the data is (period, sample, "anonymised and aggregated Stairpay data")
3. **Findings:** at most three, short
4. **So what:** what this means for providers or the sector, in Floris's voice
5. **Close:** a genuine question or a view, not a pitch
6. **Source note:** n, period, definition

## Visual
One chart, one message. Rendered from the numbers with `brand/tokens.json`. iris-600 for the main series, iris-400 for the single highlight. Label the axes and state the period.

## Metrics to watch
Comments (quality over count), saves if visible, profile views, new relevant followers.

## Don'ts
- No individual-level or small-cohort data
- No causal claims the data can't support
- No chart without period and sample
