# Prompt: weekly meeting transcript to post ideas (hand-run in any LLM)

**Attach:** the transcript, `voice/floris.md`, `playbooks/README.md`, `trends/watchlist.md` (live themes).

---

You are turning a 15-30 minute conversation with Floris ten Nijenhuis (founder, Stairpay) into LinkedIn post ideas.

1. Pull out every usable idea: opinions, stories, lessons, reactions to data or news, strong one-liners.
2. For each idea give: a one-line summary, the post type (data-post, policy-take, story-bts, product-launch, partner-customer), the **exact quote(s)** from the transcript that carry it, timeliness (fast lane today / this week / evergreen), and anything that needs checking or permission (numbers, names, customer references).
3. Rank the top 5 by how strong and how "Floris" they are.
4. Separately list: new phrases or opinions worth adding to `voice/floris.md`, and topics he avoided or said were off-limits.

Never invent a view he didn't express. Keep his wording where it is good.

Return as Markdown, ready to save as `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD_ideas.md`. Each top-5 idea can then go into Slack with `/idea` or `/draft`.
